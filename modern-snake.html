ABOUT THE GAME

A modern version of the classic Snake game

Eat the golden food to grow the snake and collect points while avoiding the walls and your own body. You start with three lives, so one collision does not end the whole game. The speed gradually increases as your level rises.

Key gameplay features

  • Three-life system with score and level preserved after a lost life.
  • Timed bonus food: hearts can restore a life and stars award bonus points.
  • Keyboard controls with Arrow keys or WASD.
  • Mouse control using Pointer Lock: click the game board once, then steer by moving the mouse. Press Esc to release the cursor.
  • Swipe and on-screen direction controls for phones and tablets.
  • English, Croatian, German, Italian and Spanish interface languages.
  • Fullscreen, sound on/off, Save & Exit, Exit without Save and Play Again.
  • Saved settings, saved game progress and a local top-ten leaderboard on the current browser.

How to play with a mouse

Select Mouse or Keyboard + Mouse in the game. Click the board once to activate Pointer Lock. The normal cursor disappears and the highlighted snake head becomes your visual reference. Move the mouse left, right, up or down to turn the snake. Press Esc whenever you want to release the mouse cursor.
