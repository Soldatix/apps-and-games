ABOUT THE APP

How the generator works

Select a date, choose a lotto format and optionally enter a Personal Key. The app deterministically derives a combination from those inputs: the same date, format, ranges and Personal Key produce the same result, so a combination can be recreated later.

The generator is intended for entertainment and convenient number selection. It does not use future draw data or a prediction method, and it cannot guarantee a result.

Preset and custom formats

Six preset formats cover common lottery structures, including formats with main and additional numbers. Custom mode lets you define the number counts and ranges for a different game instead of being limited to one national lottery.

Features and controls

  • Deterministic, repeatable combinations based on the selected date and settings.
  • Six preset lotto formats plus Custom number counts and ranges.
  • An optional Personal Key for an additional personal input.
  • History for up to 30 saved combinations, with a Copy action for quick reuse.
  • English, Croatian, German, Italian and Spanish interfaces.
  • Light, Dark and System theme choices.
  • Backup and Restore for locally stored app data, plus Reset stored data.
  • Installable PWA support and offline use after the first successful load.

Why use a date-based generator?

Some players like number combinations connected to birthdays, anniversaries or other memorable dates. A date-based generator provides a structured way to create such combinations without manually selecting every number. Because the result is generated from defined inputs, the process is more consistent than simply pressing a random button repeatedly.

Important lottery note

Date Lotto Generator does not predict future lottery draws and does not improve the mathematical odds of winning. Every valid combination in a fair lottery has the same chance of being drawn. Use the app as a number-selection aid and for entertainment only.

Privacy and local data

Your settings, Personal Key and saved history stay in your browser's localStorage. No account is required, and user combinations are not stored on an Apps & Games server. Backup and Restore let you move or retain your data yourself, while Reset stored data clears the app data saved in that browser.

Using the app

Open the generator, choose a date and one of the six presets or a Custom format, optionally add a Personal Key, and generate the combination. Copy it directly or keep it in History. After the first successful load, the PWA can also run offline on a supported browser.
