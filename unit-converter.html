Unit Converter

Fast conversions for everyday and technical units

Unit Converter is a free browser app for quick conversions across 17 useful categories and 115 measurement units. It combines everyday conversions with technical, energy and digital units in a clear multilingual interface.

ABOUT THE APP

What Unit Converter does

Enter a number, select the source and destination units, and the converted value is shown immediately. Both value fields can be edited, making it easy to calculate in either direction without rebuilding the conversion.

The app includes length, time, volume, area, mass, temperature, speed, energy, power, pressure, angle, frequency, fuel economy, force, torque, digital storage and data transfer rate conversions.

Main advantages

  • 17 practical categories with 115 measurement units.
  • Every unit name is translated with the selected interface language.
  • English, Croatian, German, Italian and Spanish interfaces.
  • Useful metric, imperial, technical and digital conversions.
  • Responsive layout for desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.
  • Free to use without creating an Apps & Games account.

Useful conversion examples

Use the app for kilometers and miles, kilograms and pounds, liters and gallons, square meters and acres, kilowatts and metric horsepower, bits and bytes, joules and kilowatt-hours, newtons, torque units and data transfer speeds.

How to use it

Open the app, choose a category, select two units and enter a value in either field. You can change the interface language at any time, and the app will update the controls, descriptions and measurement unit names.

Privacy and availability

Apps & Games does not require an account to open Unit Converter. Conversion calculations run directly in the browser. Availability and appearance can vary slightly depending on the device, browser version and screen size.
