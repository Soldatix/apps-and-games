ABOUT THE APP

Emojis with clear meanings

Browse smileys, people, animals, food, activities, travel, objects, symbols and country flags. Every item includes a concise description so you can understand its usual meaning before copying it.

The app is useful when you know the idea you want to express but are not sure which emoji fits best. Category browsing and search help narrow the choice before you copy anything.

Copy and combine

Click an emoji to copy it immediately, or add several emojis to your collection and copy the complete combination at once. Favorite and recently used emojis are stored on your device for faster access later.

Where Emoji Copy & Paste is useful

Use copied emojis in messages, social posts, comments, documents, notes and online forms. The combination tool is handy when you want to prepare several symbols together before pasting them into another app.

Favorites are useful for symbols you use repeatedly, while the recent list makes it easy to reuse something you copied a moment ago without searching again.

Country flags in Microsoft Word

Some Windows programs display Unicode country flags as two-letter country codes. For this reason, flag cards can copy a real PNG image for pasting into Word, while a separate copy option remains available for the standard Unicode flag emoji.

This gives you two choices: use the normal Unicode flag when the destination supports it correctly, or use the PNG version when you need the flag to appear as an image.

Use the right flag format in Word

Find the desired country in the flag category and click its copy button to copy the flag as a PNG image, then paste into Word with Ctrl+V (Windows). If the app reports that it copied text instead, try a browser that supports copying PNG images to the clipboard. Clipboard image support also depends on the destination application.

An image can be resized without changing the surrounding text; preserve its aspect ratio. A Unicode emoji is text and is more suitable for short messages when the receiving app displays flags correctly. Check the final document before sharing or exporting it.
