ABOUT THE APP

Emojis with clear meanings

Browse smileys, people, animals, food, activities, travel, objects, symbols and country flags. Every item includes a concise description so you can understand its usual meaning before copying it.

Copy and combine

Click an emoji to copy it immediately, or add several emojis to your collection and copy the complete combination at once. Favorite and recently used emojis are stored on your device for faster access later.

Country flags in Microsoft Word

Some Windows programs display Unicode country flags as two-letter country codes. For this reason, flag cards can copy a real PNG image for pasting into Word, while a separate copy option remains available for the standard Unicode flag emoji.

  • Spanish

Designed for every screen

The responsive interface works on desktop computers, tablets and phones. It also includes light and dark themes, category navigation, search, favorites and recently used emojis.
