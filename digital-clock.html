ABOUT THE APP

What Digital Clock does

Digital Clock gives you a large, customizable time and date display together with alarms, countdown timer, stopwatch, world clock and saved profiles. The same project is available as a web app and as dedicated Windows and Android applications.

The Windows version adds native desktop integration, Bedside mode, a system tray icon, built-in update checks and an optional Digital Clock Screen Saver. The Android version is designed for phones and tablets and includes Night mode with OLED protection.

Main advantages

  • Large and customizable digital time and date display.
  • Windows, Android and web versions.
  • Alarms, countdown timer, stopwatch and world clock.
  • Saved profiles, colors, fonts and display controls.
  • Free to use without creating an Apps & Games account.

When it can be useful

Digital Clock works well as a bedside clock, desk clock, full-screen time display, secondary-monitor clock or a simple always-visible clock on a phone or tablet.

Choose your version

Use the web version instantly in a browser, download the Windows installer for native desktop features and Screen Saver support, or install the Android APK on a phone or tablet.

Privacy and availability

Apps & Games does not require an account to use Digital Clock. Settings and profiles are stored locally on the device where supported. For information about analytics, advertising and privacy choices on this website, see the Privacy Policy.
