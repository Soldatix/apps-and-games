ABOUT THE GAME

How to play

Choose a computer level or two players on one device; select a piece and a legal destination.

Game features

  • Six AI levels, including bundled local Stockfish at level 6
  • Two players on one device with optional camera flip
  • Legal chess rules, undo, save and load
  • Five languages, installable PWA

Online Multiplayer — Web/PWA and Windows V1.2.0

Play across browsers and Windows PCs: White creates a private room and shares its 10-character code; Black joins. Both players need internet for online play. No Apps & Games account is required. Both V1.2.0 clients support online multiplayer and rematches with automatic color swaps. Windows V1.2.0 also improves reconnection and stores multiplayer session credentials in Windows Credential Manager. Microsoft Store manages updates for the Store edition. Windows Portable checks the Apps & Games version registry in Info; installation of updates is manual.

Offline and installation

Web/PWA V1.2.0 supports private online multiplayer, including against Windows V1.2.0. Internet is required for online play; offline AI and local two-player modes still work after caching. Stockfish level 6 needs its engine files cached.
