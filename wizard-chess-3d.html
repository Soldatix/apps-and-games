ABOUT THE GAME

How to play

Choose a computer level or two players on one device; select a piece and a legal destination.

Game features

  • Six AI levels, including bundled local Stockfish at level 6
  • Two players on one device with optional camera flip
  • Legal chess rules, undo, save and load
  • Five languages, installable PWA

Online Multiplayer — Web/PWA and Windows V1.2.0

Play across browsers and Windows PCs: White creates a private room and shares its 10-character code; Black joins. Both players need internet for online play. No Apps & Games account is required. Both V1.2.0 clients support online multiplayer and rematches with automatic color swaps. Windows V1.2.0 also improves reconnection and stores multiplayer session credentials in Windows Credential Manager. In the Windows Info panel, Check for updates uses Microsoft Store services.
