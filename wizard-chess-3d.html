ABOUT THE GAME

How to play

Choose a computer level or two players on one device; select a piece and a legal destination.

Game features

  • Six AI levels, including bundled local Stockfish at level 6
  • Two players on one device with optional camera flip
  • Legal chess rules, undo, save and load
  • Five languages, installable PWA

Online Multiplayer — Web/PWA and Windows V1.1

Play across browsers and Windows PCs: White creates a private room and shares its 10-character code; Black joins. Both players need internet for online play. No Apps & Games account is required. Both Web/PWA V1.1 and Windows V1.1 support online multiplayer.

Offline and installation
