Survive more UFO Invaders waves using bunkers

Bunkers are temporary protection, not a permanent safe zone. Learn to move between safe positions.

Step by step

  1. Start a round and observe the incoming shots and the condition of the bunkers.
  2. Use intact cover briefly and move when the cover is damaged or the enemy pattern leaves an opening.
  3. Keep an escape route and choose power-ups only when collecting them does not expose you to avoidable fire.

Example

Example: if the centre bunker becomes unreliable, move towards another usable position before the next wave of shots.

Tip

Compare the wave reached, not only the score. Movement and cover timing matter more than staying still.
