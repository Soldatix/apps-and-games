Internet & Connectivity · Mobile Network Coverage

Compare mobile operators along your daily route

A good signal at home does not guarantee a good connection at work. Use the map to create a shortlist.

Step by step

  1. Open the nPerf coverage map and choose the country and first operator.
  2. Inspect home, work and a few stops along your usual route at similar zoom.
  3. Switch operator and repeat. Note where data is missing and compare measured speeds separately.

Example

Example: Operator A has data near home and work, while B has a blank area near the station. Test B in person before ruling it out.

Tip

Crowdsourced measurements are not a guarantee of indoor reception or future speed.
