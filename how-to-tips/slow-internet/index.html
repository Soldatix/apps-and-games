Internet & Connectivity · Internet Speed Test

Find the cause of slow internet in three tests

Is the connection slow everywhere or only in one room? Compare like with like before calling your provider.

Step by step

  1. Close downloads and run the nPerf test close to the router.
  2. Repeat on the same device in the room where the problem occurs.
  3. If possible repeat using Ethernet. Compare download, upload and latency rather than just one number.

Example

Example: 180 Mbps near the router, 25 Mbps in the bedroom and 190 Mbps by cable points toward a local wireless problem, not proof of a provider fault.

Tip

Repeat at another time of day. A single result cannot establish the cause.
