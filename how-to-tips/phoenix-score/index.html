Improve your Phoenix Arcade score with safer movement

A risky power-up or extra shot can cost a life. Practise staying alive while scoring steadily.

Step by step

  1. Play one round and note the wave where you lose control.
  2. In the next round, move in shorter horizontal bursts and leave room to evade diving enemies.
  3. Collect a power-up only when the path is reasonably clear; compare your score and wave with the first run.

Example

Example: if chasing a bonus near the edge causes repeated losses, skip it for one round and compare how far you get.

Tip

Practise one change at a time. An individual run may depend on enemy patterns and bonuses.
