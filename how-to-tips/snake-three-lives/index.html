Use your three lives wisely in Modern Snake

The snake gets longer and faster as you progress. Make space before chasing points.

Step by step

  1. Start a game using your preferred supported controls and follow a broad route around open areas.
  2. Before collecting golden food or timed bonus food, check that you have an exit route away from walls and your own body.
  3. After losing a life, identify the turn that trapped you and avoid repeating it during the remaining lives.

Example

Example: skip a timed bonus deep in a narrow gap when your tail blocks the exit; return to a wider area.

Tip

A missed bonus can be less costly than a collision. Compare runs on the same input device.
