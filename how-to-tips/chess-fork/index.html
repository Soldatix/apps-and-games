Learn to spot a knight fork in chess

A fork attacks two targets at once. A knight is especially useful because it jumps over pieces.

Step by step

  1. Start a two-player game on one device in Animated 3D Chess and play from the normal starting position.
  2. Before each knight move, inspect the squares it could attack after landing. Look for a king and another valuable piece.
  3. When an opportunity appears, check whether the destination is legal and whether the opponent can capture your knight.

Example

Example: on an illustrative board, a white knight on e6 attacks a black king on g7 and a black rook on f8. A fork does not guarantee material if the knight can be captured.

Tip

Use undo in a practice game to compare alternatives. Do not assume arbitrary position setup is supported.
