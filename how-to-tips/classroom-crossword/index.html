Build a classroom crossword around one topic

Turn a lesson into a short recall activity with clues written for your learners.

Step by step

  1. Choose a topic and prepare six to ten short answers with clear clues.
  2. Enter the words and clues in Crossword's custom creator and check the generated intersections.
  3. Solve the puzzle yourself, then print or save a PDF for learners; keep the solution separate.

Example

Example: a geography lesson with RIVER, DELTA, LAKE, COAST, ISLAND and OCEAN, each with a one-sentence definition.

Tip

Use original clues and test spelling. Avoid clues with several equally valid answers.
