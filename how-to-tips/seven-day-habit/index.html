Try a seven-day habit challenge

Start with a tiny action you can repeat, rather than a long list of ambitious goals.

Step by step

  1. Create one recurring habit in Habit Tracker and select the days on which it should appear.
  2. Attach it to something you already do, such as breakfast, and mark completion only after doing it.
  3. After seven days, review which days worked and adjust the action or its timing.

Example

Example: read one page after breakfast every day; five completed days show where the routine already fits.

Tip

A missed day is information, not failure. Seven days is an experiment, not a promise that a habit is formed.
