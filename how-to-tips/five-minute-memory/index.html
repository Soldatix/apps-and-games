Education & Learning · Memory Game

Practise matching pairs for five minutes a day

Use a short, repeatable game session to practise noticing and recalling positions.

Step by step

  1. Choose one theme and difficulty in Memory Game and keep them constant for a week.
  2. When a card is revealed, associate its symbol with a row or nearby corner.
  3. Play for around five minutes and compare your own attempts using time or moves where shown.

Example

Example: remember the apple as top-left and the cup as bottom-right rather than guessing from the whole board.

Tip

Change only one difficulty setting at a time. Game improvement is not evidence of medical or general memory benefits.
