Convert American recipe measurements without guessing

An American recipe may mix ounces, Fahrenheit and volume units. Convert each quantity according to what it measures.

Step by step

  1. Identify whether the quantity is mass, volume or temperature and choose the matching category in Unit Converter.
  2. Select the exact source and destination units, distinguishing US from imperial volume units when offered.
  3. Convert each value and record the rounded result alongside the original recipe.

Example

Example: 350 °F is about 177 °C and 8 weight ounces are about 227 g. These are different conversion categories.

Tip

Do not convert cups to grams without knowing the ingredient density. Oven settings and kitchen measurements can be approximate.
