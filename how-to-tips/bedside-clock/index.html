Turn an old tablet into a bedside clock

Give a spare tablet a useful second life without purchasing a dedicated clock.

Step by step

  1. Open Digital Clock in the tablet browser and select a large, readable time display.
  2. Try the available dark or bedside presentation and lower device brightness to a comfortable level.
  3. Check the tablet's screen timeout and test the display before relying on it overnight.

Example

Example: put the tablet on a stable stand facing the bed and choose a dim display that remains readable from your pillow.

Tip

Use a sound alarm you have tested separately; the browser may suspend timers. Avoid damaged batteries, overheating and charging under bedding.
