ABOUT THE APP

About this app

Create scannable QR codes for Wi-Fi access, website links, text, e-mail, international telephone numbers, SMS drafts, vCard contacts and GPS locations.

What you can do

  • Eight QR types
  • PNG and SVG downloads, printing and image copying where supported
  • Six interface languages: English, Croatian, German, Italian, Spanish and French
  • Light, dark and system themes; responsive mobile layout

How to use it

Choose a QR type, enter the details and click Generate. Scan the result with your phone before sharing or printing. For locations, you can enter coordinates or request your device location with permission.

Privacy and security

QR payloads are generated in your browser; the generator has no advertising, analytics or external scripts. Only language and theme preferences are saved. Location access requires your permission; Google Maps receives coordinates only if you open its link.
