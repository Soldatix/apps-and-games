ABOUT APPS & GAMES

Free Browser Projects in One Place

Apps & Games is an independent collection of browser-based apps and games published under the Soldatix name. The goal is simple: provide useful small apps and entertaining games that are easy to open, understand and use.

Instead of requiring a separate installation, the projects are designed to run online in a web browser whenever possible. The collection combines practical apps such as Digital Clock, Date Lotto Generator and Emoji Copy & Paste with browser games such as Tetris, UFO Invaders: Neon Defense and Phoenix Arcade.

Each project is developed as a standalone experience with a focus on clear controls, responsive layouts and straightforward navigation. New apps, games and improvements can be added over time while existing projects continue to receive fixes and usability updates.

What you can expect

  • Free access to the available browser apps and games.
  • No Apps & Games account required to start using the projects.
  • Fast access from modern desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.
  • Clear descriptions so you know what each project does before opening it.
  • Ongoing improvements, compatibility fixes and new projects over time.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the apps and games free to use?

Yes. The apps and games listed on Apps & Games are free to use. Donations are optional and help support development and the project.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can open the available apps and games without creating an Apps & Games account.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The projects are designed to run directly in a compatible modern web browser. Some projects may also offer browser installation features such as a home-screen shortcut or PWA support.

Does Date Lotto Generator predict winning lottery numbers?

No. Date Lotto Generator creates repeatable number combinations from the selected date and your chosen settings. Lottery drawings are random, so the app cannot predict results or guarantee a win.

Can I use the site on a phone or tablet?

Most projects are designed for modern desktop and mobile browsers. The exact layout and performance can vary depending on screen size, browser version and device capabilities.

Does Apps & Games use analytics or advertising?

The website uses Google Analytics to understand site usage and may use Google AdSense for advertising. More information is available in the Privacy Policy.
