ABOUT THE GAME

A modern neon maze chase

Eat every dot to complete the level while avoiding the ghosts. Large power pellets temporarily turn the tables and let you chase ghosts for bonus points. The speed gradually increases as you advance, so later levels require quicker decisions and better route planning.

The neon presentation gives the classic maze-chase idea a more modern look with 3D-style characters, bright maze walls and animated bonus effects while keeping the objective easy to understand.

Key gameplay features

  • Modern 3D-style Pac-Man, ghosts, maze walls and animated bonus effects.
  • Keyboard, WASD, touch controls and full-screen play.
  • English, Croatian, German, Italian and Spanish interface languages.
  • Separate controls for sound effects and original retro background music.
  • Save and exit, exit without saving and play-again options.
  • Saved settings and a local top-ten leaderboard on the current device.

Scoring, levels and leaderboard

Your score grows as you clear the maze and use power pellets to turn nearby ghosts into temporary scoring opportunities. Finishing a level moves you into a faster round, so a good result depends on both safe movement and choosing the right moment to use each power pellet.

The local Top 10 leaderboard keeps the best results on the current device. It gives you a simple target to beat without requiring an online account or public profile.

Saving and returning later

If you want to stop playing, use Save & Exit to keep your current progress and continue later on the same device and browser. You can also leave without saving when you do not want the current run to replace the saved game.

How to play

Hold the arrow keys or W, A, S and D to move, then release them to stop. On mobile, use the direction pad or swipe across the maze. Watch the ghosts before entering narrow paths and use power pellets strategically when several ghosts are close enough to create a scoring opportunity.

Desktop and mobile play

On a desktop or laptop, keyboard controls provide the quickest movement. On phones and tablets, the touch direction pad and swipe controls allow the same maze to be played without a physical keyboard. Full-screen mode can provide more room for the game on smaller displays.
