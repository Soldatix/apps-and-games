ABOUT THE GAME

A modern neon maze chase

Eat every dot to complete the level while avoiding the ghosts. Large power pellets temporarily turn the tables and let you chase ghosts for bonus points. The speed gradually increases as you advance.

Key gameplay features

  • Modern 3D-style Pac-Man, ghosts, maze walls and animated bonus effects.
  • Keyboard, WASD, touch controls and full-screen play.
  • English, Croatian, German, Italian and Spanish interface languages.
  • Separate controls for sound effects and original retro background music.
  • Save and exit, exit without saving and play-again options.
  • Saved settings and a local top-ten leaderboard on the current device.

How to play

Hold the arrow keys or W, A, S and D to move, then release them to stop. On mobile, use the direction pad or swipe across the maze. Use power pellets strategically when ghosts are close.
