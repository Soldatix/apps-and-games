Habit Tracker / Daily Planner

Build routines, plan your day and see your progress

Habit Tracker / Daily Planner is a free browser productivity app that combines recurring habits with a practical daily task planner. Track completions, streaks and seven-day progress while keeping your personal data locally in your browser.

ABOUT THE APP

Habits and daily planning in one place

Create recurring habits, choose the days when they should appear and mark them complete as you go. The Planner section lets you add individual tasks with a date, optional time, priority and notes.

Main features

  • Recurring habits with weekday schedules and custom icons.
  • Daily tasks with date, time, priority and notes.
  • Complete and undo actions for habits and tasks.
  • Seven-day progress statistics and streak tracking.
  • English, Croatian, German, Italian and Spanish interfaces.
  • Light, dark and system theme options.
  • JSON backup export and import.
  • Local browser storage with no Apps & Games account required.
  • PWA support for installation from compatible browsers.
  • Responsive layout for desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.

Privacy-first local storage

Your habits, tasks, settings and progress are stored locally in the browser on your device. Use the backup tools in Settings if you want to save a copy or move your data to another browser.

How to use it

Add a habit and choose its schedule, or open the Planner to create a dated task. Mark items complete during the day and use the statistics view to follow recent progress and streaks.

Open instantly

No separate Apps & Games account is required. Use the button above to open Habit Tracker / Daily Planner in a compatible modern browser.
