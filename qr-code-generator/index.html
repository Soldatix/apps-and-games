A note about Wi-Fi privacy

A Wi-Fi QR code contains credentials that anyone who scans it can use. Do not publish private network QR codes publicly. WEP is outdated, and WPA3-only QR support varies by device.

About QR Code Generator

Supported QR formats

Wi-Fi, website URL, text, e-mail, phone number, SMS, vCard contact and GPS location. Download as PNG/SVG or print.

Scanning and compatibility

QR codes contain a standard string. The scanner decides whether to connect, call, compose mail, add contacts or open a map.

Your privacy

Input is processed only in this browser. We do not send or store QR content. A saved QR image can reveal the information to anyone who sees it.

Offline and advertising

The QR engine is bundled locally. There are no ads or analytics on this page; the banner area is a placeholder only.
