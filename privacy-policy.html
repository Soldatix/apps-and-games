Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Apps & Games is a website providing free online apps and browser-based games.

This policy also covers the Apps & Games applications described in Section 12, including supported web, Windows and Android editions. Website analytics, advertising and consent practices described in this policy apply where those services are used. Application-specific local data practices are explained separately below.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit appsandgames.org, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

Apps & Games does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the website.

However, certain technical and usage information may be collected automatically by third-party services used on this website.

3. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 for optional visit measurement only after a visitor has agreed to website analytics through the Privacy choices control. Without this choice, our website code does not load the Google Analytics tag.

Google Analytics may collect information such as browser type, device type, approximate geographic location, visited pages, session information, interactions with the website, and similar technical information.

Google Analytics may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies to measure website usage and performance.

Apps & Games does not intentionally send personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or payment details to Google Analytics.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

This website may use Google AdSense to display advertising.

Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies, device identifiers, local storage, or similar technologies to serve, measure, personalize, and report advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.

4.1 Adsterra Banner Advertising

On the Apps & Games home page, optional banner advertisements may be supplied by Adsterra (Ad Market Limited / ADMEDIA LLC FZ). Adsterra code is not requested until a visitor has explicitly allowed advertising through the website's shared privacy choices. Choosing not to allow advertisements does not restrict access to our apps or games.

When you allow Adsterra ads, Adsterra and participating advertising and measurement partners may receive technical data (including IP address, approximate location, browser/device information, page URL and ad interactions) and may use cookies, pixels, or similar identifiers to deliver, measure, personalize or protect advertising. Some data may be processed outside Switzerland, the UK or the EEA. For details and controls, see Adsterra Privacy Policy and Adsterra Cookies Policy.

Advertising consent is optional and independent of Google Analytics consent. The website uses a single privacy notice with separate analytics and advertising preferences; the current advertising provider is Adsterra. New or materially changed advertising partners will be disclosed and consent recollected when needed. We store your choices in your browser's local storage and a first-party cookie scoped to appsandgames.org, shared with its subdomains, for up to 180 days or until you change them. The cookie contains your analytics/advertising choices, partner revision and choice time; it does not identify an account. It lets approved Apps & Games subdomains honor the same choices without another pop-up. Use the Privacy choices control on supported pages at any time to reject or withdraw consent. Withdrawing advertising consent reloads the page and prevents subsequent Adsterra script loads. Previously stored third-party identifiers may require removal using your browser's privacy or site-data controls. The final provider partner list and detailed retention practices are determined by Adsterra and its partners.

5. Consent Management

Privacy notices and consent controls depend on the service and platform in use. Website consent controls, where configured, are separate from the controls of installed applications. We do not treat a website consent choice as consent for an Android application.

A single website privacy interface offers Accept all, Reject all and Settings. Settings allows visitors to select analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (currently Adsterra) independently. Both are disabled unless expressly permitted. Preferences are kept in local storage and a shared first-party cookie for up to 180 days, so one decision can apply to approved appsandgames.org subdomains. They can be changed or withdrawn through the persistent Privacy choices control on participating pages. Clearing browser data for appsandgames.org and its subdomains removes the stored choices. Declining either purpose does not restrict access to Apps & Games, its apps or games. This choice does not enable Google AdSense, which remains inactive pending approval and a suitable certified consent management platform.

A Google AdMob European regulations consent message for Date Lotto Generator Android is currently saved as a draft; it has not been published or shown by the released Android app. Planned Android advertising and consent controls are described in Section 12.2.2.

12. Apps & Games Applications and Local Data

Apps & Games publishes applications for the web, Windows and Android. The following information explains how Digital Clock and Date Lotto Generator handle data used by their application features. When you visit their web versions or the Apps & Games website, the website-related disclosures in this policy also apply where the relevant services are present.

12.1. Digital Clock

Digital Clock does not require an Apps & Games account. Depending on the edition and features used, the application stores preferences, saved appearance profiles, alarms and selected world-clock cities locally on your device. The web edition uses browser storage.

Digital Clock supports JSON backup export and import. A backup is created when you choose to export your data. Import reads a backup file you select and processes its supported contents to restore application data. You control where exported backup files are kept and whether to move, share or delete them.

12.2. Date Lotto Generator

Date Lotto Generator does not require an Apps & Games account. It stores the selected language, theme and up to 30 saved lotto combinations locally on your device. A saved History entry includes its date, number settings, generated numbers, creation time and the optional Personal Key used for that entry. The Personal Key is saved in History when you choose to save the combination.

In the Windows edition, application data is stored locally using the application's WebView2 storage. Saved combinations and Personal Keys are not sent to Apps & Games servers. When you choose Backup / Export, the application writes a JSON backup to the location you select. Import reads a JSON backup file you select. The application's data-reset function clears its saved History, language and theme, but does not delete backup files you exported to other locations.

The Windows Store edition of Date Lotto Generator does not include Google Analytics, Google AdSense or cryptocurrency donation options. It offers optional links to Apps & Games and to PayPal and Stripe for voluntary donations. Those links open external websites, whose operators may process information under their own privacy policies. Donations do not unlock additional app features.

12.3. Date Lotto Generator for Android — Advertising and Privacy Choices

The standard Android edition of Date Lotto Generator stores app settings and saved lotto combinations locally. The current standard Android build does not initialize Unity LevelPlay advertising or send ad requests. Testing of advertising and consent takes place only in a separate, explicitly enabled developer test application.

A consent-only Android test build may contact Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) service to request applicable privacy information and display consent choices. This form-only test does not request or display advertisements. The consent service may process technical information about the device, app and network connection to determine which privacy message is applicable and to process the user's choices.

If banner advertising is enabled in a future Android release, the app may use Unity LevelPlay, including ironSource, together with the advertising partners actually enabled in its mediation configuration. Depending on the enabled services, region, device settings and lawful permissions, these providers may process IP address, device or app identifiers, technical device information, consent preferences and advertising events for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The app's saved lottery combinations and Personal Keys are not provided to advertising networks as app content.

Where applicable, the Android app will request or refresh privacy choices using a consent management interface before eligible ad requests. Users may refuse optional consent and manage or withdraw their choices through the application's privacy options when an advertising-enabled version is introduced. The actual partner list, disclosures and app-store data declarations will be reviewed before any public advertising-enabled Android release.

Relevant third-party privacy information: Google Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy.

12.4. Your Control of Application Data

Locally stored application data remains on your device unless you choose to export, move, share or synchronize it using other software or operating-system services. Exported backups remain in the locations you choose until you manage or delete them. Uninstalling an application may remove its application-specific local storage, depending on the operating system and installation method. Operating-system backup and synchronization services are governed by the settings and policies of their providers.
